See where everyone is in the room.

A Home Assistant card that draws your mmWave presence radar in 3D: each person, their trail, your zones and a model of your room, live.

Add to Home Assistant Read the docs

Three Hi-Link radars

It reads them through ESPHome and draws what each one can actually measure.

HLK-LD2450

Position of up to three people on the floor, their speed and the sensor's zones.

HLK-LD6004

Position and height of up to three people, so each figure stands, sits or lies down. On the wall or the ceiling.

HLK-LD2410

Distance only, drawn as a shell of the beam, with the energy of each gate against its threshold for tuning.

Your own room, in 3D

Load a model of your room and say where the sensor is. People, zones and coverage are drawn inside it, and from outside it reads like a doll's house. Draw it as it is, with its textures, or as a hologram that keeps the shapes.

No model yet? Two to four photos are enough: the repository has the prompt an AI agent follows to build one, the way the living room above was made.

More than a live view

Edit zones on the floor

Drag a zone's corners or draw a new one, and the card saves it straight to the sensor.

Replay the last day

Play back 1, 6 or 24 hours from Home Assistant's history, skipping the time nobody was there.

See where people spend their time

A heatmap of the last hours, coloured by person.

Tap anyone for their history

A person or a zone opens its entity's details in Home Assistant.

It also has a visual editor, follows your Home Assistant theme, speaks English and Spanish, and stops drawing when it's off screen. See every feature.

Install it

  1. Add the card with HACS

    Open it in HACS, choose Download and reload the browser. Without HACS, copy the file from the latest release.

  2. Set up the sensor in ESPHome

    Keep the entity names from the example YAML for your sensor, so the card finds them.

  3. Add the card to a dashboard

    Search for mmWave 3D Card in Add card, or paste this and adjust the prefix:

    type: custom:mmwave-3d-card
device: ld2450
prefix: living_room_radar
title: Living room
mount_height: 2.1

Open and checked

MIT licensed. It runs in your browser, sends nothing anywhere and only changes your sensor when an admin edits its zones.

  • Every change runs CodeQL, ESLint, fuzzing, unit and browser tests before it can merge.
  • Every release is built by GitHub Actions and signed, so you can check where your copy came from: gh attestation verify mmwave-3d-card.js --repo dortizesquivel/mmwave-3d-card
  • Found a security problem? Report it privately.
